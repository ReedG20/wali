§01 — How it works

Three parts. No magic.

A pendant that hears, a memory that understands, and a quiet voice that knows when to speak up. That's the whole machine.

01 / HearPendant

It listens, and knows who's talking.

Three mics wake on speech and sleep on silence. Everything is transcribed with speaker labels, then the audio is dropped.

YOUI keep saying I'll call Dad this week.
MAYAYou said that in October.
YOU…Fair.

§04 — Privacy

Privacy is a row of switches, not a PDF.

Every setting that matters is a switch you can see, in the app and on the object. Defaults are the cautious ones.

Audio storedNever. Transcribed on device, then dropped.
ProcessingOn-device first. Anything that leaves is end-to-end encrypted.
CameraOff until you slide the shutter open. The LED tells everyone.
Other peopleNot remembered until you introduce them. Strangers stay strangers.
PauseHold the button for 2 seconds. The mics are cut in hardware.
DeleteOne button deletes everything. No exit survey.
