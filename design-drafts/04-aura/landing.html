How it works
See it in use

It listens, it learns,and then it speaks up.

Mind hears your day the way a good friend would. It notices who you were with, what you said you'd do, and how you sounded doing it.

Over weeks, that becomes a memory of your people, your goals and your patterns. It uses that memory to nudge you, and when you let it, to act for you.

The pendant

Small enough to forget. Honest about when it's on.

Privacy

Your mind is yours.
We just help you read it.

Heard on your phone, not on our servers

Transcription and mood reading run on your phone. Raw audio is deleted within the hour unless you ask Mind to keep a conversation.

Nothing acts without your rules

You decide what Mind can do alone, like a reminder or a calendar hold, and what always needs your yes, like a message or a payment.

Forget anything, any time

Say "Mind, forget that" or delete a person, a day or everything. Deletion is permanent and takes effect within minutes.
