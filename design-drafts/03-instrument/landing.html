01 — Signal

Your day, as a signal. Not a mood journal you forget to fill in.

Mind reads tone, pace, who you're with and what you're doing, then reduces it to three numbers you can actually use. They update every minute. They never need a check-in.

05 — Privacy

Built like a flight recorder. Only you hold the key.

Something that listens all day has to earn it every day. So the defaults are strict, the ledger is public, and the off switch is physical.

Hold for two seconds. It goes dark.The light goes out and the microphones lose power. Not muted in software: disconnected.

Raw audiokept 0 s

Transcribed on the pendant, then discarded. Never uploaded.

Transcripts and memoryyour keys

End-to-end encrypted. We can't read them, and can't hand them over.

Other people's wordsauto-redact

Non-consenting speakers are summarised, never quoted, after 24 hours.

Cameraoff by default

Per-moment opt in. A second LED shows when it's on.

Model trainingnever

Your data doesn't train anything but your own Mind.
