How it works

Wear it. Live your day. Let it notice.

No new habit to build and no app to babysit. The pendant does the listening; the companion does the remembering; you decide what happens next.

Moments

Built for the small moments that add up.

    The pendant Ø 31 MM · 8.4 MM DEEP
    Hardware

    Made to be forgotten on a cord.

    No screen, no buzzing, no glow across the dinner table. A smooth stone you stop noticing by lunchtime, and a light only you can see.

    Object

    • Ceramic face
    • Brushed steel rim
    • 11 g with cord
    • Linen or leather cord

    Senses

    • 2 beam-forming mics
    • Camera off by default
    • Physical shutter
    • Press to mute

    Battery

    • 3 days listening
    • Wireless dish charger
    • Full in 70 min
    • Splash resistant

    Private by design, not by policy.

    Something that hears your day has to earn that. So we built the limits into the hardware, not the terms of service.

    01

    Audio never leaves the pendant

    Speech becomes text on the device. The recording is discarded within seconds.

    02

    You can read every memory

    Everything Mind remembers is browsable, editable and deletable, one at a time or all at once.

    03

    Actions wait for you

    Anything that sends, spends or books asks first, until you decide it doesn't need to.

    04

    One press to stop

    Press and hold. The light goes out and it stops listening. No app required.
